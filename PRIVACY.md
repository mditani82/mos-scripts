# Mo's Scripts: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Mo's Scripts does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **No data leaves your computer.** The extension talks only to the Mo's Scripts helper installed on your own Mac,
  through Chrome's native messaging. It makes no network requests.
- **Stored locally only.** Your list of scripts, their output logs and the time each was last run are kept on your
  computer (in `~/.mos-scripts/` and the extension's local storage). They are never sent anywhere.
- **No analytics, tracking or third parties.**
- **Your scripts** run on your Mac with your own permissions. Anything a script itself does, such as connecting to
  a server, is up to the script you chose to run.

Removing the extension and running `uninstall.sh --all` deletes all of this data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mditani82/mos-scripts/issues
